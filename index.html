Planning rules

Company shutdown

Weekdays in a shutdown are charged to your own leave (public holidays are not). Click a shutdown day on the calendar to switch it between AL, LSL or unfunded.

Public holidays

Verify against SafeWork SA before relying on these. Entries marked unverified (2028) are best guesses. Part-day holidays (from 7pm) do not reduce leave cost.
